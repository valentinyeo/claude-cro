# Local Service CRO Template

Industry template for local service businesses. Applies to plumbers,
electricians, HVAC, dentists, lawyers, restaurants, salons, cleaning services,
home repair, medical practices, and any business serving a geographic area.

---

## Key Conversion Goals

| Goal | Priority | Typical Page |
|------|----------|-------------|
| Phone call | P0 | All pages, especially homepage and service pages |
| Contact form submission | P0 | Contact page, service pages, landing pages |
| Direction/map click | P1 | Contact page, Google Business Profile |
| Online booking/scheduling | P1 | Service pages, booking page |
| Quote/estimate request | P1 | Service pages, landing pages |
| Email signup | P2 | Blog, homepage |
| Review solicitation | P2 | Post-service, email |

## Common CRO Patterns for Local Service

1. **Click-to-call prominence** -- Phone number in header, sticky mobile call
   bar, large tap targets. Phone calls are the highest-intent conversion for
   most local services. The phone number should be on every page, always
   tappable on mobile.

2. **Service area pages** -- Dedicated pages for each city/neighborhood
   served. "Plumber in [City Name]" with localized content, testimonials from
   local customers, and service area map.

3. **Before/after galleries** -- Visual proof of work quality. Particularly
   effective for home improvement, landscaping, dental, beauty. Real photos
   outperform stock images dramatically.

4. **Emergency availability** -- "Available 24/7" or "Same-day service"
   prominently displayed. For emergency services (plumbing, locksmith, HVAC),
   this is often the deciding factor.

5. **Response time promises** -- "We respond within 30 minutes" or "Free
   estimate within 2 hours." Specific timeframes outperform vague "fast
   service" claims. Must be honored.

6. **Google review integration** -- Display Google review count and star
   rating on the website. Link to Google reviews. For local services, Google
   reviews are the most trusted source.

7. **License and insurance display** -- Show license numbers, insurance
   certificates, bonding information. Critical for trades where customers
   worry about liability.

8. **Neighborhood trust signals** -- "Serving [Area] since [Year]", local
   chamber of commerce membership, BBB rating, local award badges.

## Quick Win Checklist

- [ ] Add phone number to header on all pages (linked with `tel:` for mobile)
- [ ] Add a sticky call bar on mobile with phone number and "Call Now" CTA
- [ ] Display Google review stars and count on the homepage above the fold
- [ ] Add "Licensed & Insured" badge near the primary CTA
- [ ] Include response time promise near the contact form ("We'll call back within 1 hour")
- [ ] Add 3-5 before/after photos to the most popular service page
- [ ] Include a simple contact form on every service page (not just the contact page)
- [ ] Add a Google Map embed with the business location on the contact page
- [ ] Display operating hours prominently (header or sidebar)
- [ ] Add schema markup for LocalBusiness (address, phone, hours, reviews)

## Recommended Test Ideas

| Test | Hypothesis | ICE Estimate |
|------|-----------|--------------|
| Header: phone number with "Call Now" vs phone number only | Adding "Call Now" text will increase calls because it creates a clear action prompt | 8.0 |
| Homepage: review count in hero vs below fold | Moving Google reviews to the hero section will increase trust and conversions because it is the first thing visitors see | 7.0 |
| Form: 3 fields vs 5 fields | Reducing form from 5 to 3 fields (name, phone, message) will increase submissions because less friction | 7.3 |
| Service page: add before/after gallery | Before/after photos will increase quote requests because visual proof reduces uncertainty | 6.7 |
| CTA: "Get Free Quote" vs "Schedule Service" | "Get Free Quote" will increase form fills because "free" reduces commitment anxiety | 7.0 |
| Landing page: add video testimonial | Video testimonial will increase conversions because it feels more authentic than text | 6.0 |
| Contact page: add "Why Choose Us" section | Adding differentiators near the form will increase submissions because it reinforces the decision | 6.3 |
| Mobile: sticky call bar vs no sticky bar | Sticky call bar will increase mobile calls because the phone number is always accessible | 8.3 |
