# SaaS CRO Template

Industry template for Software-as-a-Service businesses. Applies to B2B SaaS,
B2C SaaS, developer tools, productivity software, and platform products.

---

## Key Conversion Goals

| Goal | Priority | Typical Page |
|------|----------|-------------|
| Free trial signup | P0 | Homepage, pricing page, feature pages |
| Demo/sales call booking | P0 | Homepage, pricing page, enterprise page |
| Freemium-to-paid upgrade | P1 | In-app, upgrade prompts, pricing page |
| Lead magnet download | P1 | Blog, resource pages, landing pages |
| Email list signup | P2 | Blog, homepage, exit intent |
| Feature page engagement | P2 | Feature/solution pages |

## Primary Metrics

| Metric | Definition | Benchmark (Median) | Top Performer |
|--------|------------|--------------------|---------------|
| Visitor-to-trial rate | Unique visitors who start a free trial | 2-5% | 7-10% |
| Trial-to-paid rate | Trial users who convert to paid | 15-25% | 30-40% |
| Demo booking rate | Visitors who book a demo or sales call | 1-3% | 5-8% |
| Pricing page conversion | Pricing page visitors who start trial/purchase | 10-20% | 25-35% |
| Feature page -> trial | Feature page visitors who reach trial signup | 3-7% | 10-15% |
| Annual plan selection | % of new customers choosing annual billing | 30-40% | 50-60% |
| Free-to-paid (freemium) | Free users who upgrade to a paid plan | 2-5% | 7-10% |

## Common CRO Patterns for SaaS

1. **Social proof from similar companies** -- Logos, testimonials, and case
   studies from companies in the visitor's industry or of similar size.
   "Trusted by 5,000+ B2B teams" with recognizable logos.

2. **ROI calculators** -- Interactive tools showing potential savings or revenue
   impact. "See how much you could save" with custom inputs. High engagement
   and qualification signal.

3. **Interactive product demos** -- Embedded, guided product tours (Navattic,
   Storylane, Walnut) that let visitors experience the product before
   committing to a trial or demo call.

4. **Comparison pages** -- "Us vs [Competitor]" pages targeting bottom-of-
   funnel search traffic. Feature-by-feature comparison with clear CTA.

5. **Reverse trial** -- Give full product access first, then downgrade to
   free tier. Users experience the value before losing it. More effective than
   traditional free tier for activation.

6. **Personalized onboarding** -- Ask role/use-case during signup, then tailor
   the first-run experience. Reduces time-to-value and improves trial-to-paid.

7. **Pricing page anchoring** -- Show highest-priced plan first or use a
   "Most Popular" badge on the mid-tier plan. Annual billing pre-selected with
   clear savings callout.

8. **Exit-intent for demo** -- When visitors leave the pricing page, offer a
   live demo or consultation instead of losing them entirely.

## Quick Win Checklist

- [ ] Add customer logos near the primary CTA on the homepage
- [ ] Change CTA text from "Sign Up" to "Start Free Trial" or "Try Free for 14 Days"
- [ ] Add "No credit card required" below trial signup CTA
- [ ] Display customer count ("Join 10,000+ teams") near social proof
- [ ] Add a testimonial with specific ROI outcome near pricing
- [ ] Pre-select annual billing on the pricing page with savings highlighted
- [ ] Add a "Most Popular" badge to the recommended pricing tier
- [ ] Reduce trial signup form to email + password only (collect company info later)
- [ ] Add sticky CTA bar on feature pages for mobile visitors
- [ ] Include a money-back guarantee badge on the pricing page

## Recommended Test Ideas

| Test | Hypothesis | ICE Estimate |
|------|-----------|--------------|
| Homepage headline: benefit vs feature | Benefit-focused headline will increase trial signups because visitors care about outcomes, not capabilities | 7.3 |
| Pricing page: 3 plans vs 4 plans | Reducing to 3 plans will increase plan selection rate because fewer options reduce decision fatigue | 6.7 |
| CTA: "Start Free Trial" vs "See It in Action" | "See It in Action" will increase clicks for enterprise visitors because it implies less commitment | 6.3 |
| Social proof: logos vs case study snippets | Case study snippets with specific results will increase trust because they provide concrete evidence | 6.0 |
| Demo page: form vs calendar embed | Direct calendar embed (Calendly) will increase demo bookings because it reduces steps | 7.0 |
| Pricing toggle: monthly default vs annual default | Annual default will increase annual plan selection because of anchoring and default bias | 7.7 |
| Trial signup: single page vs 2-step | 2-step (email first, then details) will increase starts because email capture reduces perceived commitment | 6.3 |
| Feature page: add interactive demo | Interactive demo will increase trial signups from feature pages because visitors experience value before committing | 6.0 |
