# Conversion Rate Benchmarks

Industry-standard conversion rate data for contextualizing CRO findings.
Use these benchmarks to set realistic targets and identify underperformance.

---

| Vertical | Avg. CVR | Top Performers | Key Conversion Action |
|----------|----------|----------------|----------------------|
| **SaaS** | 3-5% (free trial) | 7-10% | Free trial signup, demo request |
| **SaaS** | 1-2% (direct purchase) | 3-5% | Paid subscription |
| **E-commerce** | 2-3% | 4-5% | Purchase completion |
| **Lead Generation** | 5-8% | 10-15% | Form submission |
| **Subscription/Media** | 3-6% | 8-12% | Subscription signup |
| **Local Service** | 8-12% | 15-20% | Call/form/booking |
| **B2B/Agency** | 2-4% | 5-8% | Contact/RFP submission |
| **Financial Services** | 2-4% | 5-7% | Application start |
| **Healthcare** | 3-5% | 7-10% | Appointment booking |
| **Education** | 3-6% | 8-12% | Enrollment/signup |
| **Travel** | 1-3% | 4-6% | Booking completion |
| **Real Estate** | 2-4% | 5-8% | Inquiry submission |

### Notes
- "Top performers" = 90th percentile of sites in that vertical.
- SaaS free trial rates are significantly higher than direct purchase because
  the commitment threshold is lower.
- Local service rates are highest because visitors typically have high intent
  (searching for a specific service nearby).

## Benchmarks by Traffic Source

| Source | Avg. CVR | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------|
| **Organic Search** | 3-5% | High intent, visitors found you through a query |
| **Paid Search (Google Ads)** | 3-6% | Intent-driven, but varies by keyword match type |
| **Paid Search (Brand)** | 6-10% | Highest intent — already know the brand |
| **Paid Search (Non-Brand)** | 2-4% | Lower intent, comparison shopping |
| **Social Media (Organic)** | 1-2% | Low intent, discovery-phase traffic |
| **Social Media (Paid)** | 1-3% | Depends heavily on targeting and creative |
| **Email Marketing** | 4-6% | Warm audience, existing relationship |
| **Direct Traffic** | 2-4% | Mixed intent — bookmarks, typed URLs |
| **Referral** | 2-5% | Quality depends on referring site |
| **Display Advertising** | 0.5-1% | Awareness-stage, lowest intent |
| **Affiliate** | 1-3% | Varies by affiliate quality and incentive |

### Traffic Quality Indicators
- If paid search CVR is below organic CVR, keyword targeting or landing page
  relevance likely needs work.
- Email CVR below 4% suggests list quality or segmentation issues.
- If direct traffic CVR is high, brand strength is good but attribution may
  be leaking from other channels.

## Benchmarks by Device

| Device | Avg. CVR | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------|
| **Desktop** | 3-5% | Highest CVR — larger screens, easier forms |
| **Mobile** | 1.5-3% | Typically 50-60% of desktop CVR |
| **Tablet** | 2-4% | Between desktop and mobile |

### Mobile Gap Analysis
The gap between desktop and mobile CVR is a key CRO indicator:
- **< 30% gap**: Mobile experience is well-optimized
- **30-50% gap**: Typical, room for improvement
- **> 50% gap**: Mobile experience is likely causing significant conversion loss
- **> 70% gap**: Critical mobile UX issues — prioritize mobile CRO

Common mobile CVR killers:
- Forms not optimized for touch input
- CTAs below the fold or too small
- Slow load times on cellular connections
- Horizontal scrolling or layout breaks
- Pop-ups that are hard to dismiss

## Landing Page Benchmarks

| Landing Page Type | Avg. CVR | Top Performers |
|-------------------|----------|----------------|
| **Hero with form (above fold)** | 10-15% | 20-25% |
| **Long-form sales page** | 5-8% | 10-15% |
| **Video landing page** | 4-7% | 10-12% |
| **Click-through page** | 3-6% | 8-12% |
| **Squeeze page (email only)** | 15-25% | 30-40% |
| **Webinar registration** | 20-30% | 40-50% |
| **Free tool/calculator** | 10-20% | 25-35% |
| **Pricing page** | 2-4% | 5-8% |
| **Product page (e-commerce)** | 2-5% | 6-10% |

### Landing Page Optimization Factors
- Pages with a single CTA convert 2-3x higher than pages with multiple CTAs.
- Social proof above the fold increases CVR by 10-20% on average.
- Reducing form fields from 11 to 4 can increase conversions by 120%.
- Video on landing pages can increase CVR by 80% (but can also hurt if
  auto-playing or slow to load).

## Seasonal Patterns

| Period | Impact | Affected Verticals |
|--------|--------|--------------------|
| **Q4 (Nov-Dec)** | +30-50% CVR lift | E-commerce, retail, gifts |
| **Black Friday/Cyber Monday** | +100-200% CVR lift | E-commerce (peak) |
| **January** | +20-40% CVR lift | Fitness, health, self-improvement, financial services |
| **Back to School (Aug-Sep)** | +15-25% CVR lift | Education, tech, apparel |
| **Tax Season (Feb-Apr)** | +20-30% CVR lift | Financial services, accounting |
| **Summer (Jun-Aug)** | -10-20% CVR dip | B2B, SaaS (decision-makers on vacation) |
| **Holiday weekends** | -15-25% CVR dip | B2B, professional services |
| **Post-Holiday (Jan)** | +15-20% return volume | E-commerce (returns/exchanges) |

### Timing Considerations for Testing
- Avoid launching A/B tests during major seasonal shifts.
- Always capture at least 2 full business cycles (14 days minimum).
- Compare year-over-year, not week-over-week during seasonal periods.
- Factor in payday cycles for B2C (month-end/beginning spikes).

## Page Speed Impact on Conversion

| Load Time | Conversion Impact |
|-----------|-------------------|
| 0-1 second | Baseline (optimal) |
| 1-2 seconds | -7% relative CVR |
| 2-3 seconds | -16% relative CVR |
| 3-5 seconds | -25% relative CVR |
| 5-8 seconds | -40% relative CVR |
| 8+ seconds | -50%+ relative CVR |

- 53% of mobile users abandon sites that take longer than 3 seconds to load.
- Each 100ms improvement in LCP correlates with ~0.5-1% CVR improvement.
- First meaningful paint under 1.5 seconds is the gold standard.
