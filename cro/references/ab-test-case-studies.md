# A/B Test Case Study Database

Published, data-backed CRO A/B test results from B2B, SaaS, and consulting landing pages.
Use these case studies to generate evidence-based test hypotheses and benchmark expected lifts.

## 1. Lead Generation Form Optimization

| # | Test Description | Control | Variant | Result | Source |
|---|-----------------|---------|---------|--------|--------|
| 1.1 | Reducing form fields (11 to 4) | 11-field form | 4-field form | **+120%** conversions | Unbounce / Imagescape |
| 1.2 | Form field count analysis (40K+ pages) | 4+ fields | 3 fields | **+50%** conversions | HubSpot (Dan Zarella) |
| 1.3 | Multi-step form vs single-step | Single 9-field form | 3-step progressive form | **+300%** conversions | Klientboost |
| 1.4 | Multi-step form with progress bar | Long single form | Multi-step with progress indicator | **+214%** form completions | Venture Harbour / Leadformly |
| 1.5 | Field label placement | Placeholder text inside fields | Top-aligned labels above fields | **+10%** form completions | CXL Institute |
| 1.6 | Removing Company and Phone fields | 5 fields (name, email, company, phone, message) | 3 fields (name, email, message) | **+34%** submissions | Unbounce / Oli Gardner |
| 1.7 | Adding phone number field | Form without phone | Form with phone field | **-5%** conversions | HubSpot |

### Key Takeaways — Forms
- 3 fields is the optimal number for maximizing conversions.
- Multi-step forms with progress bars dramatically outperform single-step forms.
- Every additional field reduces conversions; phone number fields are particularly costly.
- Top-aligned labels outperform placeholder-only labels (users lose context when typing).

## 2. Headline Optimization

| # | Test Description | Control | Variant | Result | Source |
|---|-----------------|---------|---------|--------|--------|
| 2.1 | Benefit-focused vs feature-focused headline | Feature-focused | Benefit-focused ("Save 30 hours/month") | **+27%** conversions | MECLABS |
| 2.2 | Specific quantified vs vague headline | "Join our growing community" | "Join 14,000+ marketers" | **+12.5%** signups | ContentVerve (CXL) |
| 2.3 | Question vs statement headline | Statement headline | Question headline | **+480%** CTR | MECLABS |
| 2.4 | First-person vs second-person copy | "Start your free trial" | "Start my free trial" | **+90%** CTR | ContentVerve |
| 2.5 | Adding value proposition subheadline | Headline only | Headline + clarifying subheadline | **+88%** conversions | MECLABS |
| 2.6 | Customer-centric vs company-centric | "We provide the best CRM" | "Manage your pipeline in half the time" | **+42%** demo requests | MECLABS |

### Key Takeaways — Headlines
- Benefit-driven headlines consistently outperform feature-driven ones.
- Specific numbers and social proof in headlines increase credibility and conversions.
- First-person language ("my") creates stronger psychological ownership than "your."
- A supporting subheadline that clarifies the value proposition is high-impact and low-effort.

## 3. CTA Button Optimization

| # | Test Description | Control | Variant | Result | Source |
|---|-----------------|---------|---------|--------|--------|
| 3.1 | Green vs red CTA button | Green button | Red button | **+21%** conversions | HubSpot (Performable) |
| 3.2 | Generic vs action-oriented CTA copy | "Submit" | "Get Your Free Assessment" | **+38%** form completions | Unbounce / Oli Gardner |
| 3.3 | Adding "free" to CTA copy | "Sign Up" | "Sign Up — It's Free" | **+28%** conversions | ContentVerve |
| 3.4 | Larger CTA button with padding | Standard-sized button | 20% larger with white space | **+32%** clicks | CXL Institute |
| 3.5 | CTA placement (complex B2B products) | Above the fold only | After persuasion content (below fold) | **+20%** conversions | ContentVerve |
| 3.6 | Urgency in CTA copy | "Get Started" | "Get Started Now — Limited Spots" | **+13%** CTR | WordStream |
| 3.7 | Lower-commitment CTA language | "Request a Demo" | "See It in Action" | **+16%** clicks | Klientboost |
| 3.8 | Sub-CTA reassurance text | No sub-text | "No credit card required. Cancel anytime." | **+17%** trial signups | CXL |

### Key Takeaways — CTAs
- Never use "Submit" — action-oriented, value-stating CTAs always win.
- Adding friction-reducing text beneath the CTA is a reliable quick win.
- For complex B2B products, place the primary CTA after persuasion content, not just above the fold.
- Button color matters less than contrast with surrounding elements.
- Lower-commitment language ("See It in Action" vs "Request a Demo") reduces friction.

## 4. Social Proof and Testimonials

| # | Test Description | Control | Variant | Result | Source |
|---|-----------------|---------|---------|--------|--------|
| 4.1 | Client logos near CTA | No logos | 5 recognizable client logos above CTA | **+42%** conversions | Klientboost |
| 4.2 | Testimonial with photo vs text-only | Text-only quote | Quote with headshot, name, company, title | **+35%** conversions | CXL Institute |
| 4.3 | Results-based vs generic testimonial | "Great product, love it!" | "We increased revenue by 37% in 3 months" | **+22%** conversions | MECLABS |
| 4.4 | Number of testimonials (1 vs 3) | 1 testimonial | 3 testimonials | **+34%** conversions | Unbounce |
| 4.5 | Star rating near headline | No rating | "4.8/5 from 2,000+ reviews" badge | **+15%** form completions | Instapage |
| 4.6 | Video testimonial vs written | Written testimonial | 60-second video testimonial | **+17%** conversions (+25% time on page) | Wistia / CXL |
| 4.7 | Social proof counter | No counter | "Trusted by 10,000+ companies worldwide" | **+18%** signups | Leadpages |

### Key Takeaways — Social Proof
- Client logos placed near the CTA are one of the highest-impact trust signals.
- Testimonials with photos, names, and titles are significantly more credible than text-only.
- Specific, results-based testimonials outperform generic praise.
- 3 testimonials is optimal; diminishing returns beyond 3-4.

## 5. Pricing Page Layout

| # | Test Description | Control | Variant | Result | Source |
|---|-----------------|---------|---------|--------|--------|
| 5.1 | 3 tiers vs 4 tiers | 4-tier layout | 3 tiers with highlighted "recommended" plan | **+25%** paid signups | CXL Institute |
| 5.2 | Annual vs monthly pricing default | Monthly default | Annual default with savings highlighted | **+30%** annual plan selection | Price Intelligently (Paddle) |
| 5.3 | Highlighting "Most Popular" plan | All plans equal weight | Middle plan with "Most Popular" badge | **+20%** selection of highlighted plan, **+12%** revenue | CXL Institute |
| 5.4 | Adding feature comparison table | Pricing cards only | Cards + detailed comparison table | **+17%** paid conversions | Unbounce |
| 5.5 | "Start Free Trial" vs "Buy Now" | "Buy Now" | "Start Free Trial" | **+58%** click-throughs | Totango |
| 5.6 | FAQ section below pricing | No FAQ | FAQ with 5-7 objection-handling questions | **+12%** plan selections | Crazy Egg / Basecamp |
| 5.7 | Removing cheapest plan (decoy pricing) | 3 plans ($9, $29, $99) | 2 plans ($29, $99) | **+15%** ARPU (-5% total signups) | Price Intelligently |

### Key Takeaways — Pricing
- 3 tiers with a highlighted recommended plan leverages the decoy effect.
- Defaulting to annual pricing with savings highlighted increases annual plan uptake without reducing total signups.
- "Start Free Trial" massively outperforms "Buy Now" for SaaS.
- FAQ sections addressing common objections reduce friction at the decision point.

## 6. Hero Section Optimization

| # | Test Description | Control | Variant | Result | Source |
|---|-----------------|---------|---------|--------|--------|
| 6.1 | Product screenshot vs abstract illustration | Abstract/stock illustration | Actual product UI screenshot | **+34%** demo requests | Klientboost |
| 6.2 | Directional cue pointing to CTA | Person looking at camera | Person looking toward the form | **+17%** form completions | CXL Institute |
| 6.3 | Full-width hero vs contained hero | Full-bleed hero image | Contained with white space | **+8%** CTA clicks | Crazy Egg |
| 6.4 | Animated hero vs static hero | Static hero | CSS animation/motion in hero | **-11%** conversions | CXL |

### Key Takeaways — Hero
- Real product screenshots build credibility; abstract illustrations do not.
- Directional visual cues (eye gaze, arrows) guide users toward conversion elements.
- Subtle animation in the hero distracts from the CTA and hurts conversions.

## 7. Above-the-Fold Content

| # | Test Description | Control | Variant | Result | Source |
|---|-----------------|---------|---------|--------|--------|
| 7.1 | Form placement (low-commitment offer) | Below fold | Above fold | **+24%** conversions | ContentVerve |
| 7.1b | Form placement (high-commitment offer) | Above fold | Below fold (after persuasion content) | **+20%** conversions | ContentVerve |
| 7.2 | Removing navigation from landing page | Full site navigation | No nav (only CTA as exit) | **+100%** conversions | HubSpot |
| 7.3 | Single CTA focus vs multiple elements | Multiple competing elements | Single CTA focus | **+33%** primary conversions | Unbounce / Oli Gardner |
| 7.4 | Adding hero sub-headline | Headline + CTA only | Headline + sub-headline + CTA | **+27%** conversions | Instapage |

### Key Takeaways — Above the Fold
- Removing navigation from landing pages is one of the highest-impact changes available.
- Match form placement to commitment level: above fold for free offers, below fold for demos/consultations.
- Maintain a 1:1 attention ratio (one page, one goal, one CTA).

## 8. Video vs Image

| # | Test Description | Control | Variant | Result | Source |
|---|-----------------|---------|---------|--------|--------|
| 8.1 | Background video vs static image | Static hero image | Auto-playing background video | **-17%** conversions | Unbounce |
| 8.2 | Explainer video on landing page | No video | 90-second explainer video | **+86%** conversions | Eyeview Digital / Wistia |
| 8.3 | Product demo GIF vs static screenshot | Static screenshot | Animated GIF demo | **+37%** CTA clicks | Klientboost |
| 8.4 | Click-to-play vs auto-play video | Auto-play | Thumbnail with play button | **+18%** conversions (+30% completions) | Wistia |

### Key Takeaways — Video
- Auto-playing background videos hurt conversions (slow load + distraction).
- Click-to-play explainer videos are high-impact — users who choose to watch are more qualified.
- Animated GIFs showing product in use are a strong middle ground between static images and video.

## 9. Long-Form vs Short-Form Pages

| # | Test Description | Control | Variant | Result | Source |
|---|-----------------|---------|---------|--------|--------|
| 9.1 | Long-form vs short-form (Crazy Egg) | Short (~1 screen) | Long-form (~20 screens) | **+30%** conversions | Crazy Egg / Conversion Rate Experts |
| 9.2 | Short vs long for simple/low-cost offer | Long-form | Short-form | **+13%** conversions | MECLABS |
| 9.3 | Long-form testimonial page (Highrise) | Short page | Long page with extensive customer stories | **+102.5%** signups | Basecamp / 37signals |
| 9.4 | Long-form with features + ROI calculator (MOZ) | Short (~500 words) | Long (~2500 words with detail) | **+52%** sales | Conversion Rate Experts / MOZ |

### Key Takeaways — Page Length
- Complex/expensive products benefit from long-form pages with detailed explanations.
- Simple/free/low-cost offers convert better with short, focused pages.
- Rule of thumb: the higher the commitment, the more persuasion content needed.

## 10. Trust Signal Placement

| # | Test Description | Control | Variant | Result | Source |
|---|-----------------|---------|---------|--------|--------|
| 10.1 | Security badges near form | No badges | Trust seal next to submit button | **+11%** form completions | CXL / Blue Fountain Media |
| 10.2 | Client logos above fold vs below fold | Logos below fold | Logos directly under headline | **+20%** conversions | Instapage |
| 10.3 | Money-back guarantee badge | No guarantee | "30-day money-back guarantee" badge | **+32%** conversions | VWO / CXL |
| 10.4 | "As Featured In" media logos | No media logos | "As Featured In: Forbes, TechCrunch..." | **+14%** form submissions | Neil Patel / Quick Sprout |
| 10.5 | Privacy reassurance text near form | No privacy text | "We respect your privacy. Unsubscribe anytime." | **+19%** form submissions | ContentVerve / Unbounce |
| 10.6 | Industry certification badges | No badges | SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR badges near CTA | **+23%** demo requests | Klientboost |
| 10.7 | Real-time social proof notifications | No notifications | "John from NYC just signed up" pop-ups | **+15%** signups | UseProof (HubSpot) |

### Key Takeaways — Trust Signals
- Place trust signals close to the conversion action (near forms and CTAs).
- Client logos and certifications above the fold are more impactful than below.
- Privacy reassurance near forms is a low-effort, reliable win.
- Real-time social proof notifications work but can annoy users if overused.

## Aggregate Benchmarks

### Unbounce Conversion Benchmark Report (2024)
- Median landing page CVR (all industries): **4.3%**
- Top 25% of landing pages: **11.7%+**
- SaaS median: **3.0%** (top quartile: 9.5%)
- B2B services median: **3.5%**
- Pages with single CTA (1:1 attention ratio): **2.2x higher** CVR
- Pages with social proof: **1.5x higher** CVR on average
- Pages with video: **4.8%** median CVR vs **3.8%** without

### WordStream Landing Page Data
- Average CVR across industries: **5.89%**
- Top 10%: **11.45%+**
- Top 25%: **5.31%+**
- Best-in-class: **27.4%+** (top 10%, certain industries)
