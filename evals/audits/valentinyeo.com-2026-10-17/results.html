E1 · High · ship + baseline

Teardown button on case-study pages

Hypothesis

Because the inne case study has no teardown button and the only exit is 'Back to Home', we believe adding the button after the summary and at the end will raise teardown clicks from readers of the case study, for visitors who reach it from the home page.

How we verify results at this traffic

Too few conversions for an A/B test means no single number proves a change. We stack weaker checks that point the same way:

  1. Compare teardown clicks and bookings for 4 weeks after the fixes against 4 weeks before
  2. Five moderated sessions with target buyers
  3. Clarity recordings of sessions reaching the case study or contact page
